Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export my reports?

Yes. All charts and data tables can be exported as CSV, PDF, or Excel files. We also provide a direct API integration for pushing data into QuickBooks or Xero.

How far back does the data go?

On the Pro and Enterprise plans, we offer unlimited historical data retention. You can compare year-over-year performance endlessly.

Can I create custom dashboards?

Yes, the dashboard is fully modular. You can pin your most important metrics—like "Today's Net Revenue" or "Low Stock Warnings"—to the very top of your overview page.
