Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need coding skills to set up the Storefront?

No coding is required. Your Storefront is automatically generated from your Puxbay inventory. You simply add your logo, choose a primary color, and you're ready to sell.

Can I use my own custom domain?

Yes. While we provide a free `[your-name].puxbay.com` subdomain, you can easily connect your own custom domain (e.g., `shop.yourbrand.com`) on our Pro and Enterprise plans.

How do I get notified of online orders?

Online orders appear instantly in your Puxbay POS queue. They trigger an audible alert and push notification so your staff can fulfill them immediately alongside in-store customers.
