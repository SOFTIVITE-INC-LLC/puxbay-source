Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my existing product list?

Yes! You can easily upload your entire catalog using our standardized CSV or Excel templates. Our system automatically handles variants, barcodes, and initial stock levels.

Do I need to buy special equipment?

No. Puxbay runs on any device with a modern web browser—PC, Mac, iPad, or Android tablet. It also integrates seamlessly with standard USB or Bluetooth receipt printers and barcode scanners.

Is there a long-term contract?

Never. Puxbay is a month- service (or annual if you prefer the discount). You can cancel, upgrade, or downgrade your plan at any time without hidden fees.

What happens if my internet connection drops?

Puxbay's POS module is built with offline-first technology. You can continue ringing up cash sales, printing receipts, and opening the cash drawer. Once back online, everything syncs to the cloud automatically.
