Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my existing inventory?

Absolutely. Puxbay provides a simple CSV import tool. You can upload thousands of products, including their variants, barcodes, and current stock levels in seconds.

How does it handle negative inventory?

By default, Puxbay prevents sales that would result in negative inventory. However, you can toggle this setting globally or per-product if you prefer to allow backorders.

Is the inventory data real-time?

Yes. Whenever a sale is completed on the POS or an order is placed on the Storefront, the inventory count is instantly decremented across your entire system.
