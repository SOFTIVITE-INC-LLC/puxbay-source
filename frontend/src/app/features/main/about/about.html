The Origins

The Story of Softvite Inc

Puxbay wasn't built in a vacuum. It is the flagship product of Softvite Inc, a technology collective founded on the principle that African businesses deserve software built for their unique economic landscape.

Starting with a small team of engineers in Accra, Softvite Inc has grown into a regional leader in enterprise SaaS. We recognized early on that the retail sector was fragmented—operating with outdated systems that couldn't keep pace with modern consumer demands. Puxbay was our answer: a unified operating system for commerce.
