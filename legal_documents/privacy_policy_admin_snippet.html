Summary: Puxbay (“we”, “us”, “our”, provided by Softivite Technologies) is committed to protecting the privacy, integrity, and confidentiality of personal and business information. This Privacy Policy details the data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights under global privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and local data protection laws.

1. Scope & Relationships

Puxbay operates a multi-tenant business commerce platform providing cloud and offline-capable Point of Sale (POS), Inventory & Warehouse Management, E-Commerce Storefronts, Business Analytics & AI Telemetry, Supplier Procurement, and Staff Operations.

When you use our services, we act in two capacities:

  • Data Controller: For account information of direct merchants, administrators, staff users, billing records, website visitors, and direct communications.
  • Data Processor / Service Provider: When processing customer transactions, customer loyalty data, order fulfillment records, and customer contact information entered by or collected on behalf of our merchants. In these cases, the merchant is the Data Controller.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account & Profile Data: Full name, business legal name, corporate identifier, email address, phone number, physical address, business tax identification, and login credentials.
  • Billing & Payment Data: Payment method details, credit/debit card tokens, bank payout details, billing address, and transaction histories (processed securely via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant partners such as Stripe and authorized payment gateways).
  • Store, Inventory & Catalog Data: Product listings, pricing, SKU barcodes, inventory counts, warehouse locations, supplier details, purchase orders, and sales receipts.
  • Staff & Role Management Data: Employee names, email addresses, assigned PIN codes (hashed), role permissions, shift logs, and audit trails.
  • Customer Support & Communications: Messages, feedback, ticket submissions, and chat interactions with our support team.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Hardware Telemetry: Device model, operating system, browser type, screen resolution, IP address, network carrier, connected peripherals (e.g., barcode scanners, receipt printers, cash drawers), and app version.
  • POS & Offline Sync Logs: Offline caching tokens, transaction queue status, sync conflict resolution logs, and timestamped sales logs.
  • Usage & Performance Telemetry: Feature engagement, navigation pathways, crash diagnostics, latency metrics, and API request logs.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Essential session cookies, authentication tokens, tenant routing cookies, and preference identifiers.

C. Information Processed on Behalf of Merchants (End-Customer Data)

  • Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, shipping/billing addresses.
  • Transaction history, order items, payment tender type, and receipt delivery preferences (SMS/Email).
  • Loyalty points balances, store credits, gift card balances, and discount redemptions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following legitimate business and contractual purposes:

  • Delivering Core Platform Services: Powering POS transactions, real-time inventory synchronization, automated stock alerts, e-commerce storefront hosting, and checkout processing.
  • Offline-First Synchronization: Facilitating offline transaction queuing in browser IndexedDB/LocalStorage and synchronizing data seamlessly upon network reconnection.
  • Business Analytics & AI Insights: Aggregating sales metrics, revenue forecasting, inventory velocity analysis, and demand planning for merchant tenant dashboards.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detecting unauthorized account access, safeguarding multi-tenant database isolation, mitigating fraudulent transactions, and enforcing role-based permissions.
  • Billing & Subscription Administration: Managing plan subscriptions (Free, Starter, Pro, Enterprise), invoicing, and usage quota monitoring.
  • Customer Service & Operational Updates: Responding to inquiries, sending critical security alerts, service disruption notices, and platform enhancement releases.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information strictly under the following controlled circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted third-party cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud), database hosting, payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, Paystack), SMS/Email notification providers (e.g., SendGrid, Twilio), and customer support platforms bound by strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).
  • Payment Gateways & Financial Partners: For processing POS transactions, e-commerce purchases, payouts, and chargeback disputes.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When legally compelled by subpoena, court order, or applicable law to protect Puxbay's rights, users, or the public.
  • Corporate Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

6. Data Security & Storage Architecture

Puxbay employs enterprise-grade safeguards designed to protect data against unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 with strong cipher suites. Sensitive data at rest (including database volumes, tokens, and backups) is encrypted using AES-256.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Strict tenant boundaries are enforced at both application middleware and database query layers to prevent cross-tenant data leakage.
  • Offline Security: Locally stored transaction caches on POS client devices are scoped strictly to the authenticated domain with secure cryptographic session tokens.
  • Audit Logging & RBAC: Comprehensive audit logs track all data access, role modifications, and administrative operations.

7. Data Retention & Disposal

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with statutory retention requirements (such as tax, accounting, and commercial regulations, typically 7 years for financial records). When data is no longer needed, it is permanently deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., EEA, UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA), you have specific statutory privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can request a complete copy of your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON/CSV) via our automated self-service endpoint or account settings.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update inaccurate or incomplete information directly in your Puxbay profile.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to statutory retention obligations.
  • Right to Anonymization: Merchants can trigger customer-level data anonymization directly within the Puxbay CRM/Privacy portal.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: You may object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise your rights, visit your Settings > Privacy & Security dashboard or email us at privacy@puxbay.com.

9. International Data Transfers

If your data is transferred outside your home country or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or equivalent adequacy decisions.
