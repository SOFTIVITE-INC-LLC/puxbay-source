Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 9, 2026 • Version: 2.4
Overview: This Cookie Policy explains how Puxbay ("we", "us", "our", provided by Softivite Technologies) uses cookies, web beacons, local storage (LocalStorage and IndexedDB), and related tracking technologies across our marketing websites, cloud portal, Point of Sale (POS) interface, and merchant storefronts.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage Technologies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. In addition to HTTP cookies, modern web applications like Puxbay utilize browser storage mechanisms (including LocalStorage, SessionStorage, and IndexedDB) to provide high-speed performance, maintain user authentication, and enable reliable offline-first POS operations.

2. Why Puxbay Uses Cookies & Storage Technologies

We use cookies and storage technologies for several vital purposes:

  • To securely authenticate merchants, staff, cashiers, and administrators.
  • To maintain tenant routing and multi-tenant data isolation.
  • To cache product catalogs, barcode registries, and sales queues for offline POS functionality.
  • To remember your preferences, such as selected store location, language, register mode, and dark/light UI theme.
  • To monitor system performance, server response times, and identify platform bottlenecks.
  • To evaluate marketing effectiveness and customer referral campaigns.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

A. Strictly Necessary & Essential Cookies

These cookies and local storage tokens are essential for the operation of the Puxbay platform. Without them, core functions like secure login, tenant switching, CSRF protection, and offline sales caching cannot function. Essential cookies cannot be switched off in our preference center.

Cookie / Storage Key Type Duration Purpose
pb_auth_token HTTP / Storage Session / 30 Days Maintains encrypted JSON Web Token (JWT) user authentication.
pb_tenant_id Cookie / Storage 365 Days Identifies active business workspace and ensures multi-tenant data routing.
pb_csrf_token Cookie Session Prevents Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) on form submissions and API calls.
puxbay_cookie_consent LocalStorage 365 Days Stores your cookie consent banner preferences and acceptance timestamp.
puxbay_pos_offline_db IndexedDB Persistent Stores offline transaction queues and barcode index for POS resilience.

B. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies allow Puxbay to remember your customized settings and provide enhanced, personalized features.

Cookie / Storage Key Type Duration Purpose
pb_theme LocalStorage Persistent Remembers light or dark mode user interface appearance preference.
pb_active_register SessionStorage Session Stores the currently assigned POS physical terminal / register hardware ID.
pb_sidebar_state LocalStorage Persistent Remembers collapsed or expanded navigation sidebar preference.

C. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies gather aggregated and anonymized data about how visitors and merchants interact with Puxbay, allowing us to diagnose performance errors, page load speeds, and optimize user experience.

Cookie / Storage Key Provider Duration Purpose
_pb_telemetry Puxbay Internal 30 Days Collects anonymized page response latencies and API execution speeds.
_ga, _gid Google Analytics Up to 2 Years Measures public website traffic, referral pathways, and page visitor volume.

D. Marketing & Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be set across our public-facing marketing pages to track campaign attribution and deliver relevant updates about Puxbay features. We do not place advertising cookies inside merchant POS or administrative tenant workspaces.

4. Third-Party Cookies and Integrations

Some third-party providers integrated with Puxbay (such as payment processing gateways like Stripe or customer support widgets) may set cookies directly on your browser to facilitate transaction authentication and fraud detection. These cookies are governed by the respective third party's privacy and cookie policies.

5. How to Manage and Control Cookies

You have full control over cookie settings:

  • Puxbay Cookie Consent Center: You can adjust your cookie consent preferences at any time using the Cookie Banner or by visiting Cookie Preferences in our website footer.
  • Browser Settings: You can configure your browser (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent authentication and disable the Puxbay POS system.
  • Do Not Track (DNT) & Global Privacy Control (GPC): We recognize and respect automated browser signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) where required by applicable law.
