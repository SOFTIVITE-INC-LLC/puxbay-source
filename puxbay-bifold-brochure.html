Enterprise Overview

Puxbay is an enterprise multi-tenant cloud commerce ecosystem uniting physical retail checkouts, turnkey e-commerce, automated direct merchant payouts, warehouse logistics, and customer intelligence.

point_of_sale 1. Intelligent Point of Sale (POS) & CDS
  • • High-Throughput Scanning: Camera-based QR & hardware laser barcode detection (<100ms lookup).
  • • Multi-Tender Split: Split across Cash, Visa/Mastercard, MoMo, QR Scan, and Gift Cards.
  • • 100% Offline Resilience: Local IndexedDB queues sales during internet outages; auto-syncs on reconnect.
  • • Customer Display (CDS): Instant dual-screen broadcast of cart items, promos, and customer wallet.
  • • Cash Drawer & Shifts: Opening float, cash drops, shift drawer auditing, and instant Z-Reports.
account_balance 2. Paystack Subaccounts & Direct MoMo Settlements
  • • Direct Payouts (0-Day): 100% of customer funds route straight to the merchant's bank/MoMo (bearer: subaccount).
  • • African Mobile Money: Native support for MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money & Bank Cards.
  • • Dynamic Scan-to-Pay QR: Real-time Paystack QR codes rendered directly on POS screen.
  • • 5-Step Setup Wizard: Instant automated bank account verification & merchant registration.
